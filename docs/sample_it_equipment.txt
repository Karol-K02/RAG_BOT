IT Equipment Rules (Example Company)

Every new employee receives a laptop and, if their role requires it, a company phone.

Company equipment must be returned to the IT department on the last day of work. The employee is responsible for lost or damaged equipment according to their contract.

Installing unapproved software on company laptops is not allowed. Requests for new software are submitted through the IT helpdesk and are usually processed within 3 working days.

Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 24 hours.
