New Employee FAQ (Example Company, 2022 - outdated)

How many vacation days do I get?
Full-time employees get 25 days of paid vacation per year.

When do I get my laptop?
Your laptop is prepared by the IT department before your first day and handed over during onboarding.

Can I work remotely?
Employees can work remotely up to 2 days per week after completing their 3-month trial period, with their manager's approval.

Who do I contact with IT problems?
Contact the IT helpdesk through the internal ticket system.
