Employee Handbook - Northwind Supplies Ltd.
Version effective from 1 March 2025

Section 1. Working hours
Employees work Monday to Friday. Core office hours are 9:00-17:00.

Section 2. Annual leave
Employees on an employment contract receive 25 days of paid leave per year.
Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 14 days in advance.

Section 3. Remote work
After the probation period, employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week, subject to their manager's approval.

Section 4. Business expenses
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the end of the trip, together with original receipts.

Section 5. Accounts and devices
Passwords for company accounts must be changed every 120 days.
A lost or stolen company device must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 24 hours.
